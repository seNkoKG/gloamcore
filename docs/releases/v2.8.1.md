# GloamCore 2.8.1

GloamCore 2.8.1 restores complete Faustus coverage for current official
exchange markets and checks for newly published completed-hour data sooner.

## Fixes

- Resolves every Faustus seed through the current PoE Wiki item metadata ID on
  both Windows and mobile. poe.ninja image texture paths are retained only as
  a last-resort fallback and can no longer override authoritative metadata.
- Shows guarded official completed-hour markets by default instead of making
  valid items appear missing. If the newest digest has no usable range, the
  most recent official observation from the eight-hour history is retained
  with its exact age. Guarded rows are excluded from movers and trends.
- Checks for a newer official Faustus snapshot every five minutes. When a new
  completed-hour digest is late, GloamCore retries once per minute rather than
  waiting for the next hour or entering a per-second request loop.

## Verification

The release was checked against the live Allflame economy and official
Faustus digest. All 990 current exchange-backed poe.ninja item names resolved
to authoritative metadata. Currency retained all 102 catalogue items in
GloamCore, including Awakener's Orb and Prismatic Catalyst; 100 had a usable
range in the newest audited hour and two shard rows retained their immediately
preceding official observation with an age warning. No invented or Ninja
fallback price is assigned.
