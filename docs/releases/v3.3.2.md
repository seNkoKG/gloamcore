# GloamCore 3.3.2

GloamCore 3.3.2 completes the Path of Exile visual system and rebuilds Atlas
Command Center from the current official GGG art layers. Its deterministic
Path of Exile graph, point, allocation, refund, gateway, and URL logic remains
unchanged.

## Authentic Atlas presentation

- The canvas now composes official inactive/active icons with the matching
  normal, notable, keystone, and wormhole frames instead of exposing square
  sprite crops.
- Official connector textures, orbit rings, cluster backplates, and the Atlas
  background render from GGG's current group and orbit geometry.
- Node art stops enlarging past its native sprite resolution, keeping high
  zoom crisp, while pan, cursor-centred wheel zoom, fit, search focus, and
  exact hit targets remain interactive.
- The selection inspector occupies a dedicated rail and no longer covers the
  tree.

## Strategy Presets

- Named trees are visible beside the Atlas and can be saved, replaced by name,
  loaded, deleted, and compared locally, up to 30 presets.
- Existing saved loadouts remain compatible. Presets continue to migrate by
  exact node ID when a validated league pack changes, and incompatible nodes
  are reported rather than guessed.

## Finished Path of Exile theme

- The Wraeclast palette now covers native market tables, filters, cards,
  League Center, Item Intel, Player Toolkit, Build Lab, drawers, settings,
  overlays, inputs, borders, hover states, and scrollbars.
- Theme-specific surfaces and text pass automated contrast checks. Gloam Teal,
  Azurite Blue, and Ember Gold are unchanged.
- Craft of Exile and Stash Wealth remain site-owned embedded browsers and are
  not recoloured.

Craft of Exile and the bounded Ctrl+D seller snapshot remain intact. This
release does not restore the retired full official-trade-listings subsystem.
