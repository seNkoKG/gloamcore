# GloamCore 2.8.0

GloamCore 2.8.0 hardens the Build Planner and Player Toolkit with exact visual
data, official market data, and a more reliable embedded stash experience.

## Highlights

- Rebuilds Cluster Back as a packaged, searchable 8-passive Large Cluster
  Jewel wheel with exact notable artwork, legal result validation, and exact
  official Trade queries.
- Corrects exact artwork selection for equipment, flasks, active and support
  gems, cluster jewels, base jewels, and Timeless Jewels.
- Adds editable item, skill, and configuration sets backed by PoB 2.67.2,
  including validated raw item edits, exact weapon-set state, 870 gem choices,
  578 configuration controls, and explicit main-skill and Full DPS selection.
- Recalculates imported PoE 1 builds in a fresh verified PoB process; the
  release parity fixture matches all 100 comparable numeric PoB output stats.
- Fits the full PoB Timeless Jewel influence radius into the passive-tree
  viewport, including edge sockets and the correct jewel-family treatment.
- Adds official Faustus completed-hour history with midpoint, range, stock,
  volume, trend, and Chaos/Divine conversion data for supported categories.
  Completed hours below 20 traded units or with a wide price range are marked
  unreliable and cannot drive default trend or mover views.
- Makes the isolated Wealthy Exile view fill the Stash Wealth workspace and
  respond to window resizing, with reliable load failure and retry handling.
- Keeps optional Regex workbench map-property controls out of the way until
  they are needed.

## Correctness boundaries

Cluster Back supports Large Cluster Jewels with exactly eight passives. Medium
and Small Cluster Jewels are intentionally excluded because they do not share
the same three-notable back-position problem, and the current official Trade
schema does not provide a complete exact-search path for every requested Small
base. Faustus values are labelled as completed-hour official exchange data,
not live quotes.
