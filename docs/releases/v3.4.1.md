# GloamCore 3.4.1

GloamCore 3.4.1 is a focused rate-safety hotfix for the bounded live seller
prices shown by the `Ctrl+D` price checker.

## Fixed

- Rapid modifier and item-filter edits now settle before starting a new search.
- Official Trade requests are serialized, and identical in-flight requests are
  shared instead of duplicated.
- GloamCore follows Grinding Gear Games' dynamic rate-limit headers and
  `Retry-After`, and locally blocks requests for the full indicated cooldown.
- The compact overlay reports the remaining Trade cooldown instead of showing
  only a generic failure.

The 30-second snapshot cache, maximum ten live seller rows, and official Trade
browser handoff remain unchanged. Craft of Exile remains intact, and the
retired full official-trade-listings subsystem remains absent.
