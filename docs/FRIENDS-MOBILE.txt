GLOAMCORE 3.2.0 — POE 1 ECONOMY + LEAGUE COMMAND CENTER ON YOUR PHONE

Android:
1. Copy GloamCore-Android-3.2.0.apk to your phone.
2. Open it and approve “Install unknown apps” for your file/browser app.
3. Launch GloamCore and allow notifications if you want price-target alerts.

What it includes:
- Every current PoE 1 economy category from poe.ninja
- Current-league detection, search, sorting, and supported category filters
- Ninja Exchange/Stash prices with source-age and stale-data guards
- Documented Public Currency Exchange completed-hour evidence from Faustus
  with five-minute discovery checks
- Item descriptions and modifiers from PoE Wiki
- Item Intel live search for items, bases, affixes, tiers, tags, and acquisition
- Exact in-game item, currency, card, gem, map, and equipment artwork
- League Navigator and the official-data Atlas planner with authentic PoE art,
  exact point rules, official URL sharing, and saved loadouts
- Snapshot-only Build Upgrade Assistant for two exported GloamCore builds;
  mobile labels stored PoB outputs non-authoritative and potentially stale
- Safe PoE Wiki, PoEDB, and Craft of Exile handoff buttons
- Price trends, liquidity, aggregate observations, confidence, trade links,
  and watch targets
- A local copied-item parser and manual-paste price checker with exact,
  similar-roll, base-only, unique-jewel, and Timeless-seed comparison plans,
  plus mapped Chronicle-room, Veiled-state, and safely calculated equipment
  property filters where the copied item supplies enough information
- A safe user-clicked official Trade handoff with mapped filters prefilled;
  mobile does not include the Windows global capture, overlay, or input helper
- Source-timed refresh, CDN-age checks, guarded thin-market estimates, and a
  strict two-hour maximum for clearly marked offline market snapshots

iPhone:
Extract the included source archive on a Mac, run pnpm install and
pnpm mobile:sync, then open the Xcode project and sign it with your Apple
account. Apple does not permit a normal unsigned IPA to be shared directly.

Privacy:
GloamCore uses bounded market and Wiki services. It does not read game memory
or automate gameplay. Mobile price checks use manual paste, send no game input,
and never use account cookies or POESESSID. No GloamCore package calls the
Trade website's undocumented search, exchange, or fetch APIs.
